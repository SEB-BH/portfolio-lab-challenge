## About

In this lab, you will build the first version of your personal portfolio website.

A portfolio is an important tool for developers. It gives you a place to introduce yourself, show your skills, and share your projects with employers or clients.

You will practice using **semantic HTML** to organize the page clearly. Then you will use **CSS** to improve the design and make the page look more professional.

By the end of this lab, you will have a simple portfolio page that you can continue improving throughout the course.
